![Universal Browser Agent conceptual illustration](../assets/universal-browser-agent.jpg)

**Focus:** Intelligent browser automation for practical digital workflows.

**Public status:** Private development

## Product overview

Intelligent browser automation for practical digital workflows.

## Intended use cases

Cross-site research, structured information gathering, repetitive administrative tasks, and supervised browser operations.

## Design focus

The product direction emphasizes clear operator control and review of consequential actions.

These are product-direction descriptions, not claims of general availability or independently verified performance.

## Availability and disclosure

This initiative is not advertised as generally available. Detailed implementations, product internals, security mechanisms, operational configurations, and private evaluation evidence are not published.
